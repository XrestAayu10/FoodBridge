How it works

One workflow, three roles

From surplus food to a rescued meal — every step is tracked, verified, and confirmed with a pickup code.

1. Register & verify

Suppliers and organizations register with a role. Organizations are reviewed by an admin before they can request food.

2. Post surplus food

Suppliers add a photo, quantity, and pickup deadline, and confirm the food is safely stored.

3. Browse & request

Approved organizations browse available listings and request a pickup.

4. Accept & get a code

The supplier accepts the request, generating a short pickup code and reserving the listing.

5. Confirm handover

Both sides enter the pickup code at collection time. Once confirmed, the listing is marked collected.

6. Track impact

Meals rescued, active listings, and verified organizations are tallied live on every impact dashboard.
